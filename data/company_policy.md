# Employee Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 24 annual leave days per calendar year. Annual leave should be requested through the HR portal and approved by the employee's manager. Employees should submit planned leave requests at least five working days in advance where practical.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 12 sick leave days per calendar year. If an employee is absent for more than two consecutive working days, HR may request supporting documentation according to company policy.

## Carry forward
A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days may be carried into the next calendar year, subject to local employment rules and manager approval. Sick leave is not carried forward.

## Public holidays
Company public holidays are announced by HR at the beginning of each calendar year. Public holidays are separate from annual leave.

## Notice period
Employees are expected to provide 30 calendar days' notice when resigning, unless their employment agreement specifies a different period. The HR team confirms the applicable notice period for each employee.
